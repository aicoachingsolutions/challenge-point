# RPC-001 vertical slice: six selections into one resolved game

17 September 2026. Paper only: no activity generation, no implementation. Representativeness and
whether the intended affordances emerge were kept out.

**The question:** can the contributions of six selected knowledge objects coexist in one coherent
resolved game without any source object needing bespoke awareness of the others?

**The answer: five of six, cleanly. The sixth fails, on one field.**

---

## What was run

Six objects each derived their minimum structural contributions across the eight areas, in isolation,
each forbidden from mentioning or accommodating the others: **RPC-001 Goalkeeper Build-Out**, **From
Goal Kicks**, **Directional Possession Games (GF2)**, **Neutral Player Condition**, **Wide Zone
Advantage**, **Variable Target Condition**. That produced 89 REQUIRED or EXCLUSION contributions.

Two reconcilers then merged all six into one resolved game, independently, from opposite directions —
one outward from the physical layout, one inward from the scoring rule. Where an authored ownership or
relationship rule resolved a clash they used it; where resolving needed a new rule they flagged it.

I adjudicated a canonical resolved game from the two, then ran **Gate A** (two blind readers) and
**Gate B** (one checker) separately, with an adversarial pass over both.

## The reconciliation converged

Independently, both reconcilers produced the same game:

| Decision | Result | How it was settled |
|---|---|---|
| Player partition | 2 goalkeepers + 4v4 outfield + 2 neutrals = 12 | Three objects that never mention each other (equal team sizes, a goalkeeper per team, neutrals drawn from the fixed total) leave one feasible split, once Neutral Player's "one or two" preference picks 2 of {2, 4} |
| Scoring event kind | `target_zone_entered` | The intersection of GF2's two events with RPC-001's four; lines eliminated because two full-width lines at one end cannot occupy distinct positions |
| Direction | One candidate set per team, opposite ends | GF2's "same direction" guidance is PREFERRED_DEFAULT and adapts; the direction invariant holds |
| Turnovers | Play stops; the winner restarts from its own half | GF2's continuous-play guidance is PREFERRED_DEFAULT; RPC-001's restart rule is REQUIRED |
| Wide-zone reward | Only the scoring multiplier survives | A bonus point would be a second way to score; a free restart would break the build-out restart |
| Restarts | Three rules, no conflict | GF2 fixes the beneficiary, RPC-001 the ball placement, From Goal Kicks the goal-kick case |

**No REQUIRED contribution was dropped by either reconciler.**

## Gate A — can it be laid out and played?

**Both blind readers: it lays out, and it cannot be played as specified.**

- **Layout and roster resolve uniquely and fit the envelope.** Width 6 + 18 + 6 = 30 m exactly; length
  20 + 20 = 40 m; 5 + 5 + 2 = 12 players; every cross-reference resolves. Reader A ran 25 checks that
  found nothing, reader B 22.
- **The blocker:** nothing in the game determines which of a team's two candidate target zones is live,
  so the only scoring event has no object at any instant.
- The readers agreed on the verdict and on the root fact. They coded one finding differently, and that
  difference is a representation gap, not reader noise: the procedures' closed effect lexicon has no
  type for an information rule, so one reader recorded it as indeterminate (correct) and the other
  forced it into `P-EFFECT`.

- **Nothing was dropped.** 0 of 89.
- **18 of 89 do not work in play:** 10 present but inoperable, 8 unresolved. (Gate B's own prose said
  17; its item counts say 18, and the item counts are right.)
- **The 8 unresolved are one failure, not eight.** RPC-001 needs the scoring objective live from the
  first instant of an attack; Variable Target forbids it being determinable before a trigger fires. That
  takes down the objective state, the information rule, the direction entry and the primary event's
  object with it.
- **Variable Target is the one selection that did not really arrive:** 6 of 15 operable.
- **Seven of the ten inoperable items are gaps the contributing object itself declared** — "controlled
  on arrival" and "long kick" have no authored threshold, the wide-zone multiplier has no magnitude.
  Those are authoring gaps, not reconciliation failures.

## Bespoke awareness: where it fired

**Once, irreducibly.** RPC-001 and Variable Target both write `objectives[].state`. RPC-001 could only
state its requirement by assuming the objective set is fixed and singular; Variable Target could only
state its own by assuming nothing else needs a live objective at the first instant. Neither could have
avoided this by being more careful. **This is content, not wording.**

**Once, half representational.** Variable Target's only authored trigger is a change in the defence's
commitment, and RPC-001 is the object that fixes where the defence starts. But `objectives[].state` is
a single prose field carrying five different things: current value, scope, persistence, assignment
function, and trigger mapping. Split those out and a total assignment rule could satisfy both
contributions at once. **The field shape is part of why they look jointly unsatisfiable.**

**Once, against a standing decision rather than a sibling object.** Variable Target's authored range of
2–3 targets is a whole-layout count written under an implicit single-direction assumption. A reciprocal
two-direction game with a varying set on both sides needs four. Fixable by authoring the range per
objective set instead of per area.

**One near-miss that survived by luck.** Wide Zone Advantage offered three possible rewards, and two
were barred by the one-primary-event rule. Had it authored only "bonus point", it would have been
unrealizable here without knowing what else was selected. **There is no way in the representation for
an object to declare a fallback.**

### What made coexistence work

In every case where independent authoring succeeded — the player partition, the three-rule restart,
the goalkeeper question, the incentive exclusions, the choice of scoring event — the thing that did the
work was **an object's own explicit statement of what it does not claim**. Coexistence held wherever
objects declared their silence, and failed where two objects claimed the same field positively.

## Representation gaps found

All inside the eight areas. None needs a ninth.

| Gap | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Transitions holds one value per slot.** A restart conditioned on who last touched the ball cannot be expressed; three compatible rules read as rivals | The out-of-play slot receives three stated values with different named teams, which are in fact orthogonal: trigger, beneficiary, ball placement |
| **`objectives[].state` is one prose field doing five jobs** | It carries current value, scope, persistence, assignment function and trigger mapping, which is why an initial-value claim and a knowability claim collide on it |
| **Rules of value has no typed slot for information** | The closed effect lexicon has no information type, so an information rule cannot be typed at all |
| **A value modifier has no magnitude field** | "MAGNITUDE UNRESOLVED" sits inside a prose string instead of an empty required field a check could see |
| **Target zones exist only as objects, never in Space** | So their extent is never laid out, and the 12 × 3 m zones are a free realization choice with structural consequences |
| **Performers has no slot for start placement** | RPC-001's first-defender line had to be smuggled in as a pseudo-performer |
| **Provenance cannot express the kind of a source** | Contribution ids, standing decisions, adaptation tokens and absence markers sit in one flat array, so a reader cannot tell which is which |

## Errors found, including my own

The adversarial pass checked both gates and my adjudication. What it found, and what I verified:

- **My canonical choice breached an authored range.** I made the symmetric version canonical, which
  instantiates four target zones against Variable Target's authored 2–3. The variant I set aside
  respected the range. The breach is mine, not the knowledge's.
- **The resolved game contains an invented consequence wearing borrowed provenance.** It states that
  entering an *inactive* candidate zone is out of play. The rule cites six contributions, and none of
  them authorizes it. Because the zones sit 3 m inside the end line and each spans 6 m of a wide
  channel, this creates play-stopping regions inside a channel that Wide Zone explicitly declares never
  entry-prohibited. **Both Gate A readers missed it.** Provenance looked complete while not supporting
  the claim — a failure mode worth naming.
- **A gate departed from its own procedure.** One reader coded the information rule with a fact code the
  procedures do not make available; the other correctly recorded it as indeterminate.
- **A reader contradicted itself.** The multiplier magnitude was marked as not blocking play, while the
  same reader's scenario ends "the running score is undefined".
- **Gate B's arithmetic slipped** (17 against its own 18) and it was both too generous and too harsh in
  places: too generous in marking the range breach "survived", too harsh in charging the reconciliation
  with gaps the source objects declare themselves.
- **One challenger claim was wrong, and the cause was mine.** It called reader A's citations of the four
  accepted corrections fabricated. The corrections were real; I supplied them to the readers and
  neglected to pass them to the challenger.

## What this establishes

- **The shared representation carried five of six selections with no bespoke awareness at all.** That is
  the architectural result.
- **The one failure is a single field where two objects each correctly authored incompatible claims.**
  It is not general incoherence, and part of it is the field's shape rather than the knowledge.
- **The mechanism that makes independent authoring work is the declaration of non-claims.** Objects that
  said what they were not claiming coexisted; the collision happened where two claimed the same field.
- **Every gap found sits inside the eight areas.** The representation needs internal structure, not more
  areas.

## Preserved as unresolved

C1 live-target assignment at the start of an attack (needs an authored rule and an owner); C2 the
wide-zone multiplier magnitude; C3 RPC-001's "controlled on arrival" and "long kick" thresholds; C4
possession continuity, which closes itself while turnovers stop play. Plus the standing list: Game Form
restart × From Goal Kicks, central weighting × Wide Zone Advantage, Turnover Reward's missing
consequence, Environmental Manipulation family-ID provenance, Counterattack timing, and the three
wording issues.

Two authored contradictions were confirmed in passing: Wide Zone Advantage says both "Mark wide
channels along both touchlines" and "Zones optional"; GF2 pairs "a directional target line or zone at
one end" with "Teams attack in the same direction".

### Activity 1: Central Corridor Possession Game

**Objective.** Create and exploit space in the central corridor to progress forward.

**Setup.** Play 6v6 with goalkeepers on a 40 x 30 m (44 x 33 yd) field. Divide the field into three zones: two 18 m (20 yd) wide channels and a central 18 m (20 yd) corridor. Teams start in their defensive third. Play begins with a goalkeeper's distribution, and restarts the same way after a score or ball out of play. Mark a line across the pitch beyond the first defenders.

**Rules.**
- Maintain possession under pressure to create scoring opportunities.
- If possession changes, quickly reorganize to regain control.
- Keep the ball and work it forward under pressure. Force it or lose it and the other team attacks straight away. Play does not stop.
- When the ball changes hands, the next action goes straight away. No reset, no stoppage.

**Scoring.** Earn a point when a player dribbles over the line, or receives the ball past it and controls it. A long kick that lands past the line does not count. Start each attack from your goalkeeper or a restart in your own half. Points are worth most through the middle, then wide, then deep.

### Activity 2: Live Transition Possession Game

**Objective.** React quickly to transitions and maintain possession under pressure.

**Setup.** Play 6v6 on a 40 x 30 m (44 x 33 yd) field. No fixed zones inside the field. Teams start in their defensive half. Play begins with a pass from the defending team, and transitions are live with immediate possession flip on turnovers. Mark a target zone beyond the first defenders. Start each attack from your goalkeeper or a restart in your own half.

**Rules.**
- React quickly to transitions when possession changes.
- Play continues without stoppage on turnovers.
- Keep the ball and work it forward under pressure. Force it or lose it and the other team attacks straight away. Play does not stop.
- When the ball changes hands, the next action goes straight away. No reset, no stoppage.
- When the ball changes hands, play carries on from wherever everyone is. Whoever lost it presses straight away.

**Scoring.** Earn a point when a player dribbles into the target zone, or receives the ball there and controls it. A long kick into the target zone does not count. Start each attack from your goalkeeper or a restart in your own half.

### Activity 3: Overload Scoring Possession Game

## 7. The decisions, in coach language

- **D1. Which Game Problem is Progress the Attack's?** Through the Game Problem Library, as his reply asks: *"that should be addressed through our existing Game Problem Library rather than assigned merely to satisfy the engine."* (31 October, line 17). Attack Development lists Create Space and Exploit Space as PRIMARY, Gain Access and Maintain Possession as SECONDARY (`LIB:660-702`); nothing ranks them. - **D2. When does an attack start?** Every possession (what composes today) or only once possession is organized? For Secure Possession he wrote *"I do not approve option (a), which would treat every possession as equivalent and produce a generic possession game."* (30 October, line 20). Does that reasoning hold for Progress the Attack? If so, the starting condition needs a sign a coach can judge and E8.
- **D3. What counts as a score?** Is the far end line the "more advanced representative area" (PR-1), or an end zone of a stated depth, or an area short of it (Library: "progression zones;", "target positioning;")? Is "while maintaining attacking possession" judged at the target (PR-2) or kept from the attack's start? - **D4. What is the other team trying to do?** Mirror the attack (as composed; RPC-003-06 assumes it from GA-001's "Reciprocal progression toward meaningful external objectives."), or defend with an objective and event of its own (PR-5)?

## 2 · How "substantive" and "explanatory" are told apart here

The Goalkeeper Build-Out record's own definitions (its §2; ours, not his), applied unchanged:

- **SUBSTANTIVE**: the item would change what the engine could build or check for Attack Development if it were in the workbook.

His Workbook Standard lists begin conditions, end conditions, representative information, degenerate solutions and behaviour adjustments as property categories of the workbook (as that record reports), so an omission in those categories is a property omission, not narrative.

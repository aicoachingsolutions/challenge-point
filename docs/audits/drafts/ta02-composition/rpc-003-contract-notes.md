## 10. Plain English

- **What works.** Attack Development can be written in the same engine-readable form as Goalkeeper Build-Out, from its own workbook rows alone, with every claim quoted, our readings labelled as ours, and no engine wording borrowed.
- **What doesn't.** As drafted, Secure Possession is refused at structure (U1). It gets through structure only with proposed rulings PR-1, PR-2 and PR-4 supplied TEST-ONLY as stand-ins and one of PROP-134's three clauses removed, and it is then refused at communication for want of his approved wording (U2); even then its "more advanced representative area" is just GF2's end line. That is not a near-success (§5). None of Attack Development's three scoring conditions can stand on the knowledge as written.
  - "From the attack's starting condition" cannot be represented until he says what that starting condition is for this goal. Its removal is a silent loss, not a near-success.
  - "While maintaining attacking possession" names no place; until he types it (as he did for Goalkeeper Build-Out), the activity is refused.
  - "A more advanced representative area" is, in practice, just GF2's end line, whatever we declare about it.
  - Both remaining conditions need his approved coach sentence before a coach can be shown them.
- **Why it matters.** Without his scoring condition, the "composed" activity is just the plain possession game. Every check still passes, so the engine cannot tell the difference. Withholding it is the honest result.
- **Does it block a pilot?** Yes, for Secure Possession. It is not a block for anything already validated.

# Design reasoning: the isolated A02 foundational test

**Standing.** Review evidence only. TEST-ONLY. Isolated: nothing a coach or an administrator can reach runs it. Not deployed. Not merged: the branch `claude/a02-foundational-test` has no pull request, and the standing auto-merge practice does not apply to it. Not an authorization, not approved wording, not a selection input. Engineering evidence only: no run here establishes ecological suitability or representative sufficiency. Christian authorized it in Christian's reply about A02, filed in the sources repository as `sources/2026-11-10-a02-authorization/originals/christian-reply-to-foundational-audit.txt`: "I authorize the next bounded, isolated A02 engineering experiment." and "This authorization is for the isolated development pathway. It does not authorize production changes or broader Stage 2 implementation." (10 November 2026)

**Dates.** The reply was pasted on 10 November 2026 without its mail header (the sources log says so), so the date Christian sent it is not recorded. The review data (`a02-inputs.review.json`) and every report of this branch cite it with 10 November 2026, the date it was pasted and filed.

**Quotation marks.** Double: Christian's words, verbatim. Single: ours (labels, values, record text, code).

## What this is

The isolated A02 engineering experiment, built in four slices on the isolated handoff of 7 November (`docs/audits/design-reasoning-handoff/`), each answering one or more of Christian's seven report items:

- **Consumption** (report item 1). One TEST-ONLY option of the checked chain's TEST-ONLY entry (`buildActivityUnderAuthorizationRecords`, `upstream`) hands it Step 1's resolved Game Problem and Step 2's SELECTED form. The chain's candidate forms become the routed context's declared forms filtered to Step 2's form (an intersection, never a replacement; a form outside them is refused); its constraints come only from the authorization record; Step 1's Game Problem is carried beside the planning model's reading, never written over it, and no decision of the chain reads the carried value (the safeguards read it CARRIED_NOT_READ: Step 1's Game Problem reaches the chain only through Step 2's form); the chain records what it was handed (`consumed`), and the handoff reads that record back.
- **High Press Escape's CANDIDATE executable interpretation** (report item 2). Our restatement of RPC-002's approved scoring events, primary scoring condition and two identity rows, 11 items each quoting its approved row verbatim, and a DRAFT A02 record (no ruling field filled, an empty set), both only in the TEST-ONLY draft store `docs/audits/drafts/a02-composition/`. Five runs: A02-0 (the knowledge as it stands), A02-1, A02-1b and A02-2 (each with labelled TEST-ONLY treatments, never the A02 configuration) and A02-D (the diagnostic: structural compatibility only).
- **The safeguards** (report item 3), **the quota** (report item 4) and **the earliest consequential blocker** (report item 5). One pure check per run (`safeguards.ts`) over 65 runs: decision ownership, dependency verification and consumption tracing, each with a test that fails when a downstream decision contradicts its authorized input or rests on an unsupported replacement; the zero-, one- and two-manipulation runs through the record route with the TEST-ONLY size-rule bypass; the stop sequence in chain order, named in Christian's failure-source categories.
- **The A02/TA02 comparison** (report item 6, brief). TA02 through the same consumption over our DRAFT restatement of RPC-003 (TA02's context pending), and one comparison test of what the two goals share and where they differ.
- **This folder** (report item 7 and the evidence): the report organized by Christian's seven items, the results, the regression record.

## How to run and regenerate

From `back/`:

- The evidence check: `node node_modules/ts-node/dist/bin.js --files -r tsconfig-paths/register ./src/system/design-reasoning/a02-evidence.unit.ts` (also in `npm test`). It fails if the committed report or results differ from what it generates.
- Regenerate: the same command with `UPDATE_A02_EVIDENCE=1` (PowerShell: `$env:UPDATE_A02_EVIDENCE='1'` first; Git Bash: prefix the command). The evidence is generated twice inside the test, the second time from a second build of the A02 cases and the TA02 runs through the chain, and must be identical; no time stamp is written.
- The other A02 tests: `a02.unit.ts`, `a02-safeguards.unit.ts`, `upstream-consumption.unit.ts` (same command, each file). Imported by the evidence test, `a02.unit.ts` and `a02-safeguards.unit.ts` run none of their own tests; run alone (as `npm test` runs them) they run all of them.
- No runner exists: the draft store cannot be loaded by a runner, so the unit tests are the only place the experiment meets the chain.

## Known limitations

- **The production route is unchanged.** It still selects A02's form from the wording (GF3) and is refused at selection there (OD-A02-LEGACY-FORM). The consumption exists only on the isolated pathway, as Christian authorized it.
- **Two readings beside the consumed ones.** The Game Problem reading that governs the engine's lens hints is still the planning model's (OD-HINT-GAME-PROBLEM), and the chain reads the context from the routing itself (OD-CONTEXT); both equal Step 1's on A02 by agreement, not by consumption. The Step 1 Game Problem the chain carries is read by no decision of the chain (CARRIED_NOT_READ, a detected disconnection).
- **Wording still reaches the selection.** The engine lenses follow the wording (OD-WORDING-LENSES), and through the kept package checks that read them, the wording decides the selection of one two-manipulation set (OD-WORDING-FLIP). Whether those checks are kept under Christian's section 5 or are a selection from wording is put to Christian in the report.
- **High Press Escape's condition does not execute.** The origin clause has no carrier (the only typed begin condition is a restart, the only episode scope the build-out one), the control referent is undeclared, the pressing line is open text, the scope is over-applied to the whole game (ours), no approved coach wording exists, and LAW-11 for A02 is unsettled. A02-1b and A02-2 pass those stops only under labelled TEST-ONLY treatments and are never the A02 configuration; A02-D shows structural compatibility only.
- **The account's display.** The chain's account says the approved condition is in the activity whenever the context's contract is read, while no clause is carried (OD-DISPLAY); the requirement account reads NOT_CLASSIFIED, never a pass: none of these runs hands the chain requirement bindings for the condition rows (none exists for RPC-002; a DRAFT set of ours for RPC-003, `docs/audits/drafts/requirement-bindings-rpc-003.DRAFT.json`, is not handed to the TA02 runs).
- **No manipulation is justified for A02** in anything held. Every one- and two-manipulation run rests on a TEST-ONLY relationship stand-in (TEST ASSUMPTION, class 4 at best). Each refusal is attributed by re-running the selector's exported package check on the run's lens pool: a reconstruction, not an observation of the selector's loop; the role mix cannot be attributed without an observer, and none was added.
- **The TA02 runs rest on a pending context.** Step 1 routes TA02 to Attack Development by the planning model; Christian has said that context does not adequately represent the moment and deferred the proposed ninth context. Our restatement of RPC-003 is a DRAFT of 27 October, unreviewed. With every condition item withheld, A02 and TA02 collapse into one plain GF2 activity; the one line that differs (a transition on a change of possession) holds the same value from two sources: in A02 the standing decision SD-14, which records Christian's decisions of 18 September; in TA02 our restatement of RPC-003 (its item RPC-003-09.a, restating the context library's rows RPC-PROP-037 and RPC-TR-007).
- **The safeguard runs are built once per process.** The evidence's second generation rebuilds the A02 cases and the TA02 runs through the chain; the 65 safeguard runs are compared on a second build of the five A02 cases by their own test.
- **The date of Christian's reply about A02** is cited as 10 November 2026, the date it was pasted and filed; the date it was sent is not recorded.
- **Isolation rests on the branch** staying unmerged and undeployed: three bundled files differ from main by the TEST-ONLY hunks above. The behaviour snapshot against main (391 entries) is the behavioural proof, recorded in the regression record.

ACTIVITY 1 of 3 — Central Corridor Possession Game   (coach entered 16 players)

OBJECTIVE
  Players decide how to maintain possession while progressing the ball through the central corridor under pressure. Success is measured by the ability to keep possession and effectively use the corridor to advance. Session focus: Maintain possession with forward intent.

SETUP
  8v8 game with two teams aiming to progress the ball through a central corridor in a 40x30 yard area. The corridor is 10 yards wide, running the length of the field. Teams start in their defensive halves, and play begins with a pass from the defending team. After a score or ball out of play, the game restarts with a pass from the defensive end.

HOW TO PLAY
  • Begin with a pass from the defensive end.
  • Progress the ball through the central corridor to score.
  • If the ball goes out or a score occurs, restart from the defensive end.

RULES
  1. Play stays live as possession is secured and progressed toward the target under pressure. A forced ball or turnover flips the immediate attacking advantage to the opponent with no reset.

SCORING
  A point or live advantage counts when the team progresses possession toward the directional target. A point or live advantage counts only when possession is maintained under pressure and the ball is progressed toward the target before the space closes. The field is treated as three value zones: regains in the central zone count higher than regains in the wide zones, and regains in the wide zones count higher than regains deep. The zone weighting stays the same in every live contest.

COACHING FOCUS
  • Observe how players maintain possession under pressure.
  • Encourage players to identify and exploit space in the corridor.
  • Focus on decision-making for progression versus possession stability.

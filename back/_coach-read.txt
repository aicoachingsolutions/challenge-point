
================ ACTIVITY 1: Central Corridor Possession Game
SETUP: Set up a rectangular field with a central corridor flanked by wide channels. Teams play 8v8 within the central corridor, with additional players positioned in the wide channels as support options. The game restarts from the central corridor after each goal or turnover.
RULES:
  - Play stays live as possession is secured and progressed toward the target under pressure. A forced ball or turnover flips the immediate attacking advantage to the opponent with no reset.
SCORING: A point or live advantage counts when the team progresses possession toward the directional target. A point or live advantage counts only when possession is maintained under pressure and the ball is progressed toward the target before the space closes. The field is treated as three value zones: regains in the central zone count higher than regains in the wide zones, and regains in the wide zones count higher than regains deep. The zone weighting stays the same in every live contest.
COACHING FOCUS:
  - Observe players' decision-making in maintaining possession with forward intent.
  - Encourage players to exploit the central corridor and use wide channels for support.
  - Focus on continuous directional circulation and progression without restricting passing options.

================ ACTIVITY 2: Live Transition Possession Game
SETUP: Create a playing area with two end zones and a central playing field. Teams play 8v8, with possession flipping immediately upon a turnover. The game continues live without stopping, and players decide to react quickly to regain or maintain possession.
RULES:
  - Play stays live as possession is secured and progressed toward the target under pressure. A forced ball or turnover flips the immediate attacking advantage to the opponent with no reset.
  - On possession change, both teams stay live in the same space with no reset: defenders re-press immediately from wherever they were standing when the ball changed hands, and play continues until the next decision resolves.
SCORING: A point or live advantage counts when the team progresses possession toward the directional target. A point or live advantage counts only when possession is maintained under pressure and the ball is progressed toward the target before the space closes. Possession kept under live pressure is scored when retention itself is the contest.
COACHING FOCUS:
  - Watch for quick adaptation to possession changes and immediate transition responses.
  - Encourage maintaining possession with forward intent while reacting to live transitions.
  - Focus on players' ability to switch play and exploit open spaces.

================ ACTIVITY 3: Overload Scoring Possession Game
SETUP: Divide the field into three zones: two end zones and a central zone. Teams play 9v7 in the central zone, with the numerical overload favoring the attacking team. Scoring is weighted, with bonus points for successful possession sequences involving all players.
RULES:
  - Play stays live as possession is secured and progressed toward the target under pressure. A forced ball or turnover flips the immediate attacking advantage to the opponent with no reset.
SCORING: A point or live advantage counts when the team progresses possession toward the directional target. Possession kept under live pressure is scored when retention itself is the contest. When a numerical advantage is held in the zone where pressure is applied, regains in that zone carry higher value: the scoring weight scales with the overload that produced the regain, and balanced-number regains keep the base value. Score is weighted by where possession changes hands: regains in a forward zone count higher than regains in a defensive zone, and the same weighting applies in every live contest.
COACHING FOCUS:
  - Observe how players utilize the numerical overload to maintain possession and create scoring opportunities.
  - Encourage strategic use of overloads and role asymmetry to progress play.
  - Focus on players' ability to execute possession sequences that involve all players for bonus scoring.

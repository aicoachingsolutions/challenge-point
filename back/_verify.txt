GOAL: Move the ball into a target zone | coach entered 12 players

--- Activity 1: Central Corridor End Zone Game
SETUP: Set up a field with a central corridor and wide channels. Each team aims to progress the ball into the opponent's end zone using the central corridor while maintaining possession. The game restarts from the central corridor after each score. Teams play 6v6.
RULES:
  - The target zone remains active for both teams throughout play. Entry under pressure keeps the next action live, and any turnover gives the opponent immediate access to attack the other way with no reset.
  - Teams score by progressing to the target or end zone.
  - Live opposition contests every attempt to progress.
  - Score awarded for plays that visibly create or open space for a teammate — stretching, unbalancing, or pulling defenders out of position so a teammate has a free option.
  - Score awarded for passes or runs that break or bypass a defensive line; line-breaking attempts that are read and intercepted hand the advantage to the opponent on the regain.
SCORING: A point counts only when the team progresses into the target zone under live opposition and keeps the next action alive. Scoring is tied to reaching or using the end zone or target area. The field is treated as three value zones: regains in the central zone count higher than regains in the wide zones, and regains in the wide zones count higher than regains deep. The zone weighting stays the same in every live contest.

--- Activity 2: Live Transition End Zone Game
SETUP: Organize a field with end zones and allow live transitions. The game restarts immediately after the ball is out of play, encouraging quick decision-making and continuous play. Teams play 6v6.
RULES:
  - The target zone remains active for both teams throughout play. Entry under pressure keeps the next action live, and any turnover gives the opponent immediate access to attack the other way with no reset.
  - Live opposition contests every attempt to progress.
  - Score awarded for plays that visibly create or open space for a teammate — stretching, unbalancing, or pulling defenders out of position so a teammate has a free option.
  - Score awarded for passes or runs that break or bypass a defensive line; line-breaking attempts that are read and intercepted hand the advantage to the opponent on the regain.
  - On possession change, both teams stay live in the same space with no reset: defenders re-press immediately from wherever they were standing when the ball changed hands, and play continues until the next decision resolves.
SCORING: A point counts only when the team progresses into the target zone under live opposition and keeps the next action alive. Scoring is tied to reaching or using the end zone or target area. A goal or bonus counts only when the team enters or uses the target end zone under live opposition.

--- Activity 3: Overload Scoring End Zone Game
SETUP: Set up a field with numerical overloads in specific zones. Scoring is weighted based on successful use of overloads and entry into the end zone. Teams play 7v5.
RULES:
  - The target zone remains active for both teams throughout play. Entry under pressure keeps the next action live, and any turnover gives the opponent immediate access to attack the other way with no reset.
  - Teams score by progressing to the target or end zone.
  - Live opposition contests every attempt to progress.
  - Score awarded for plays that visibly create or open space for a teammate — stretching, unbalancing, or pulling defenders out of position so a teammate has a free option.
  - Score awarded for passes or runs that break or bypass a defensive line; line-breaking attempts that are read and intercepted hand the advantage to the opponent on the regain.
SCORING: A point counts only when the team progresses into the target zone under live opposition and keeps the next action alive. Scoring is tied to reaching or using the end zone or target area. When a numerical advantage is held in the zone where pressure is applied, regains in that zone carry higher value: the scoring weight scales with the overload that produced the regain, and balanced-number regains keep the base value. Score is weighted by where possession changes hands: regains in a forward zone count higher than regains in a defensive zone, and the same weighting applies in every live contest.

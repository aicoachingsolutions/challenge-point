WHICH EXISTING FIELDS CAN ALREADY CARRY PART OF IT

More than I expected, which is why the proposal is small:

- **Its initial value is already a realization freedom.** SD-R2: "which team starts can remain a permitted
  free choice unless selected knowledge requires otherwise". So the representation must carry a governed
  choice here, not compute one — and the realization layer already carries governed choices.
- **Its change already has an event.** POSSESSION_CHANGE is a registered trigger, and a transition carrying
  it is how the corpus says a change happened.
- **The episode relationship needs nothing new.** SD-14 already names the events that begin an attacking
  episode, and `startsEpisode` already marks them. Worth noting SD-14 names **three** — START, SCORE and
  POSSESSION_CHANGE.
- **`ATTACKING_TEAM` already means the current value.** It simply resolves against nothing today.


WHAT GENUINELY NEW REPRESENTATION IS REQUIRED

One game-level relation: which team currently holds the ball. Initial value a governed choice, change bound
to the existing trigger, and `ATTACKING_TEAM` resolving against it instead of against nothing.

What it must **not** include, so it stays the size the corpus supports: no scoring eligibility, no objective
association, no account of how possession is gained or lost, no post-score rule, and no attacking/defending
label on a team.


THE THREE CONCEPTS — and A04 already proves your point

You asked me not to assume team-in-possession, team-eligible-to-score and team-associated-with-an-objective
are the same. They are not, and in A04 **the identity is already false** rather than merely unestablished.
GF2 authors J3 as "EACH_TEAM: one shared target attacked by both", so in the game we have been closing:

    team associated with the objective   neither, or both — one target, attacked by both
    team eligible to score              both, continuously
    team in possession                  one at a time, and the only one that discriminates

Which is also the clearest statement of why the unsupported `designation` had to go: collapsing the three
would have forced us to call one of A04's two teams "the defending team" in a game where both attack the same
line.

Your single-goal check-out example survives the proposal, because the proposal says nothing about scoring
eligibility — so it does not foreclose a structure where possession and scoring eligibility separate in time.
I added no machinery for it and used it only as the diagnostic you offered it as.

THE ONE THING I MOST WANT YOU TO SEE

This check exposed an unresolved authoring decision in existing knowledge, with nothing to do with Wide Zone.

**SD-14 says a SCORE begins a new attacking episode. SD-R3 says the post-score procedure has no universal

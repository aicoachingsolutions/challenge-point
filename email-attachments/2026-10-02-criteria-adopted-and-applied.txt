THE LEARNING-RELEVANT CONCERN

The chain holds, and it is the reason Wide Zone is the shaping constraint here: central density makes the
centre scarce, the penetration opportunity moves wide, the channel makes it legible and valuable, a defender
has to come out, and that is where the 1v1 becomes available. The reward points at the right landscape and
leaves the solution entirely open.

But the condition is satisfied by **controlling the ball in the space** — not by engaging a defender and not
by penetrating. A team could circulate into the channel and back out, never attempt the 1v1, and still take
the bonus. The incentive rewards *finding* the space rather than *using it against someone*.

I do not think that is a contradiction. An incentive that *required* a 1v1 engagement would be the more
dangerous design, and exploit_space is this constraint's authored target rather than "beat a defender". But
it is a real gap between what is rewarded and what the session is for, and it bears directly on magnitude —
an argument for a multiplier that biases attention rather than one large enough to pay for circulation.
Which is a second reason your deferral of magnitude was right.


THE STRUCTURAL FINDING — and it reaches the issue you separated

"Attacking team" cannot be evaluated without something the representation does not establish. To know a touch
was the attacking team's, the game must establish either:

  - which team is attacking at the moment of the touch — live possession attribution, which I cannot find
    established; or
  - that the touch belongs to the team that subsequently scored — determinate at scoring time without
    possession attribution, but it needs a window tying the touch to the score, which is the episode scope.

So the two are not independent. You asked me to keep the episode-scope issue separate and not to invent an
attacking-episode representation to support this incentive, and I have invented neither. But the
attacking-team qualifier makes that scope load-bearing for THIS condition rather than merely desirable.

Your restriction is well-reasoned and the criteria support it — a defensive clearance should not arm the
attack's bonus. What I am reporting is that the separation you intended may not be available: the condition
cannot be fully authored until one of those two is settled.

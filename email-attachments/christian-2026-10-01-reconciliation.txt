2 · THE THREE-WAY DISTINCTION IS ALREADY IN THE MODEL, AND YOU ARE RIGHT ABOUT
    OBSERVING

Verified against the existing representation, nothing added.

P11 is "the realized participation state of a performer GROUP". A participation
state therefore exists only for a group that is in the game. So a role available
in the session but not established by the selected knowledge is simply NOT
INSTANTIATED — no P4 roles[] member, no P11 entry — rather than instantiated and
marked OBSERVING. Your expectation is what the model already says.

OBSERVING is for a group that IS in the game and is not currently participating.
Using it for an unavailable or uninstantiated role would have conflated "not
here" with "here and resting", which are different facts about the game.

So the three levels are:

  available in the session     session context — DID NOT EXIST, now added
  instantiated into this game  P4 performers.teams[].roles[], P3 for the count
  participation state          P11/P12, only for an instantiated group

role names are sport knowledge and the engine knows none of them. There is no
Team Profile concept anywhere today; this is the pathway one would populate, with

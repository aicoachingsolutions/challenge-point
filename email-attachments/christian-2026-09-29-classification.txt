PATTERN 1 — NINE LINES ASK A TURNOVER WHOSE END LINE THE BALL CROSSED

The corpus has four transitions. Three are POSSESSION_CHANGE. For all three, the
line trace is identical: T2, T3, T4 and T5 are WITHDRAWN, and T1a, T1b, T1c
fail.

That is the whole finding. The mechanism that recognises an inapplicable
transition property already exists and already works — a CONTINUE transition has
no restart, so "who restarts" and "from where" are correctly withdrawn on
exactly these elements. T1a/T1b/T1c are simply not covered by it, so a turnover
is asked whose last touch put the ball out, whose end line it crossed, and in
which region. A turnover has none of those.

The knowledge objects say so themselves. GF4: "No last-touch qualifier is
authored." A01-02: "every other out-of-play case is free." Wide Zone: "start and
restarts after score, out of play and turnover free." Pass Combination Gate: "No
transition is authored."

And the only "cannot author" declaration reaching those rows is A01-02's, which
is about its own goal kick — a transition A01-02 in fact resolved. Declarations
reach by row, so a gap declared about one element is charged to every element on
that row.

One unresolved rule — which qualifier rows apply to which trigger — not nine
knowledge gaps. The register already expresses the same idea in prose for the
neighbouring rows: T3, T4 and T5 carry "N/A when CONTINUE" in their valueType.
Nothing equivalent exists for T1a, T1b, T1c.

If these must be authored, every object has to gain a metric vocabulary it

AFFORDANCE LENSES — YOUR BROADER TEST CHANGES THE ANSWER, AND I WAS WRONG

Seventeen lens selections are realized only by the game form. You were right not
to assume only constraints can realize a lens, and my 19-of-39 overstated the
problem by a factor of three.

The link is exact rather than interpreted: all three libraries already write the
same snake_case affordance key, so a lens's category normalises to what a
constraint writes in targetAffordancePrimary and a game form writes in
primaryAffordances.

The six undetermined cases are all one defect: the vocabularies have drifted. A
game form holds "fast_attack" and "regain"; lenses produce "attack_quickly" and
"regain_possession". Calling those unrealized would assert that fast_attack is
not attack_quickly, which is a semantic claim I am not entitled to make in
either direction.

That drift is the real selection-integrity finding. Five of the nine affordances
a lens can ask for cannot be named by one of the two libraries at all — no
constraint can name finish, maintain_possession or protect_space; no game form
can name attack_quickly or regain_possession.

So: adopt the invariant, but reconcile the three vocabularies to one closed list
first. Otherwise it measures vocabulary drift rather than causal integrity.

Practice Situations cannot participate at all — the record carries ID, parent,
display order, name and a prose Definition, and no field in which a causal
contribution to a lens could be recorded.


REASON CODES — A PROPOSAL THAT CHANGES NO BLOCKING BEHAVIOUR

One code per declaration, and a precedence in which a statement always outranks
a silence:

  1  NOT_AUTHORED            declared gap            needs it, cannot author it
  2  EXCLUDED                excluded                states it must not exist
  3  NON_CLAIMED             not constrained         looked, imposes no requirement
  4  CLAIMED, still failed   claimed but unresolved  claimed, no value survived
  5  UNDECLARED              coverage                looked and said nothing
  6  nothing reaches it      no coverage             nobody looked

An explicit exclusion is never reported as though nobody considered the
property, and a non-claim is never represented as missing authorship by that
object. Every line that fails today still fails with the same verdict; only the
reported reason changes. Whether "not constrained" or "excluded" should stop
blocking realization is the separate decision you reserved.

Neutral count — the answer is no, and I am glad you asked before converting.

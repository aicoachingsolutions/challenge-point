TWO FINDINGS WORTH HIS TIME

1. Learning-Relevant passes WITH A CONCERN. The ecological chain genuinely holds — central density makes the
   centre scarce, the opportunity moves wide, the channel makes it legible, a defender comes out, 1v1 becomes
   available. But the condition is satisfied by controlling the ball in the space, not by engaging anyone. A

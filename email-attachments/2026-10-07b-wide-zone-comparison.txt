THE SMALLEST COUPLING, AND WHY IT COSTS NOTHING EXTRA

Your entitlement test, applied to the settled formulation rather than to the bare reading: the
formulation itself does not create an entitlement. "A touch within the channel after which the touching
team retains possession" is grained to one action. What creates the indefinite entitlement is that
nothing in A04 bounds the advantage once earned — the modifier is a bare region predicate with no scope
at all. So the unbounded reading is an artifact of our representation, not of your condition.

And the smallest bound is already inside your condition. It reads possession — "after which the touching
player's team retains possession". The same relation bounds the advantage: it applies while that team
still has the ball, and a possession change ends it. That needs

  - no episode model, general or otherwise;
  - no time window;
  - no new dependency, because the condition already reads the relation that would bound it;
  - and nothing A04 does not already hold: it has the possession relationship and one transition, keyed
    on the possession change.

I will note without leaning on it that this is also what our own generator has been saying in unratified
wording for months: "your team keeps it until the next change of possession." That sentence was filed as
a ratification candidate and could not rest on anything. It can now.

Three things you should weigh against it.

  1. IT FLIPS THE SD-104 EXEMPTION. Your ruling two days ago was that an initial state value is
     activity-design information when an established rule reads it, and that A04's initial possession
     holder is exempt because none does. Author this condition and one does. The holder would become
     activity-design information, the coach would have to be told who starts, and the Q3 report we just
     agreed to leave visible would become a genuine loss rather than an exempt one. That is a consequence
     of your two rulings read together, not a new question.

  2. NOTHING CAN CARRY IT TODAY. A value modifier has exactly five fields — condition type, condition
     referents, magnitude, operation, combination — and not one of them can say what meets the condition
     or name a scope. I checked every row in the register: none has a criterion, a parameter or a
     satisfied-by field. The smallest addition is a new field on the existing modifier collection, which
     is the same size of act as the operation field you ruled in under SD-30, and the register's own note
     calls that "a new field on an existing collection, so LOCAL by the grammar sheet's own test".

  3. A WINDOW IS NOT THE ANSWER, AND I CHECKED. A time window opens on an event by construction, but it
     CLOSES on time only — the expiry field says what happens when it expires, not what ends it. So
     "until the next change of possession" is not expressible as a window, and a modifier cannot
     reference a window in any case.

The one alternative that needs no new field at all: read the condition as satisfied when the scoring
event itself occurs inside a channel. Coupled cannot fail, because the qualifying interaction and the
score are the same occurrence. I am not recommending it, for two reasons. It is the least faithful of the
candidates — scoring in a channel is neither starting in nor moving through — and it is not as free as it
looks, because the channel widths in the artifact are an upper bound rather than a marked width, so "the
score happened inside a channel" is not determinate on the ground without the coach fixing the width
first. It is worth having as the fallback if you decide the new field is too much before field use.

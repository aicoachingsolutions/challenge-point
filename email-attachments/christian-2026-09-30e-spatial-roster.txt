A THIRD SILENT LOSS, FOUND ON THE WAY

The width had been authored and was never leaving the engine. `bounds` was
emitted only on an open line, and Wide Zone authors the channel width on the SAME
ROW as "touchline-adjacent" — so a line with a derived position dropped its
authored extent entirely. Position and extent are two authored facts on one row.

Now emitted under its own name, `extentBounds`, so the invariant that `bounds`
appear only on an open line survives: a bound there is a choice space, this is an
extent beside a settled position. Different things, different names.

That is the third instance of this shape in this work, and all three were found by
something downstream trying to use the value rather than by a test.

2 — WHAT I AUTHORED, AND WHY IT STILL CANNOT BE EVALUATED

Authored: the modifier exists on V7 with a region condition, magnitude 2, operation MULTIPLY. Both
values come from the registered closed lists, and SD-30 is explicit that a magnitude without an
operation is incomplete, so the pair is authored together. The base value is untouched, so the ordinary

This is the first time V7 has carried an item. The consequence is immediate and good — the object's
claims about the advantage now reach lines instead of vanishing, which is what my 1 October note said

But realization is now refused, and the refusal is correct:

    not, because: a pre-realization Gate A invariant cannot be evaluated, so it is not satisfied:
    GA-MODIFIER-OVERLAP

The modifier's referents are the authored prose "both wide channels of this contract, each a referent".
SD-58 forbids comparing open text as identity, because that would promote text equality into identity.
So the overlap invariant cannot evaluate, and a game whose invariant cannot be evaluated is not
authorized.

Typing the referents is the canonical remedy — your SD-98 precedent, same operation as the typed bounds
in C29c and C30b. **It has no working form today, and that is the finding.** I tried both:

  · one item whose value is an array of two typed references — the array is read as a permitted SET, so
    the line became "choose one of the two channels", which inverts the authored "both";
  · two items, each typing one referent — they collide under SD-02, two support-capable items on one
    line with nothing authored to decide between them.

V8b's registered valueType says "references to regions, objects or events; **one property per
referent**". Neither route implements that. So "a condition on two referents" is currently not
representable, and I reverted both attempts rather than leave a collision in the corpus that I had
created rather than found.

Worth noting what made me confident the typing itself was legitimate: after your C33 restatement, "both
wide channels of this contract" names exactly two items determinately. Before it, the same sentence
answered to three classes and typing it would have required choosing between them. Your ruling is what
made the reference well-defined — the representation is what cannot carry it.

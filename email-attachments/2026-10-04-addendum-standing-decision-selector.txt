THE DEFECT

**A citable standing decision's registered selector is never read.** The function that decides whether a
standing decision applies to a line matches on the **row only**. It reads the decision's row, splits it, checks
the line's row is among them, then checks three guards — the session, an existing entailment, and an optional
condition. It never looks at the decision's `selector`.

Three citable standing decisions register a selector:

    SD-13   row T3   selector  trigger=START
    SD-14   row T7   selector  trigger ∈ {START, SCORE, POSSESSION_CHANGE}
    SD-20   row T6   selector  trigger=POSSESSION_CHANGE

All three selectors are ignored.


WHAT IT ACTUALLY COSTS — one wrong value, and I checked all thirteen goals and every practice situation

SD-13 and SD-20 never apply anywhere, because an authored item pre-empts them and the entailment guard holds.
So the cost is confined to SD-14, which applies exactly three times:

    GF2-07.a        trigger = POSSESSION_CHANGE        correct
    GF4 I06         trigger = POSSESSION_CHANGE        correct
    A01-02-01.a     trigger = OUT_END_LINE             WRONG

**The goal-kick transition receives `startsEpisode = true` from SD-14, and SD-14 names only START, SCORE and
POSSESSION_CHANGE.** `OUT_END_LINE` is not among them. So the engine is asserting something SD-14 does not say,
on a real transition, in A01 under practice situation A01-02.

I want to be careful about what I am and am not claiming. I am **not** claiming a goal kick does not begin an
attacking episode — that may well be true of football. I am claiming the representation has no authority for it

*"Defines boundaries only; entails no T1 element."* A value with no source is the defect, whether or not it

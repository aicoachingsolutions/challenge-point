2 — startsEpisode AND space.axis: NOT RENDERED, with your rule encoded

Your rule is in the code as two conditions, not one, because the second is the one that does the
work. Claiming a property is accounting metadata is cheap; proving its operational consequences
reached the coach is not. So each exclusion names the paths carrying its consequences and the checker
verifies that an instruction actually cites them. startsEpisode discharges its one consequence —
that play continues on a turnover — via the playState instruction. space.axis declares none, which
is only defensible because the rendered geometry is already in metres.

Tested by breaking it: remove the "play continues" instruction and the startsEpisode exclusion stops
being permitted and becomes a violation. So the list cannot be used to quietly drop something
load-bearing.

Both exclusions are printed in the output with their reason and their discharge, as you asked.


3 — THE THREE CHANNELS: your first case, and the contract already told us what it needs

They are co-referential. Nothing authored differentiates them: all three derive the identical
position values, and the one authored item that fixes the number states "**both** wide channels of
this contract". The authored count on S2 is 2, stated twice as a requirement.

So the intent is two channels, one per touchline — and the contract knew it could not say so. Its
own ledger entry reads:

    "touchline-adjacent does not name which touchline and S2 has no side attribute, so 'one on each
     lateral side' is unheld; COUNT 2 alone allows both channels on one side. Needs a side selector
     attribute or per-touchline relative terms."

**The register now has that attribute.** S2 carries a `lateral` selector attribute whose draft closed
list under AM-17 includes `wide-left` ("touches the 0 touchline and does not contain the midline")
and `wide-right` ("touches the W touchline"). And AM-12 is explicit that derivation never rewrites an
authored selector to use them, so the contract gains nothing from AM-17 until it is restated.

So the distinction is authorable and not authored. It does not need inventing, and I have not
invented it. What it needs is a restatement of the Wide Zone S2 contributions to carry
`lateral: wide-left` and `lateral: wide-right` — which is canonical knowledge, and therefore yours.

That route also resolves three-into-two without touching the class model. I would avoid an
engine-side merge: the current behaviour is ruled, not accidental. One element class is minted per
contributing item, and there is a test asserting "classes are never merged, even where their
selectors coincide", citing SD-47. Merging would overturn that, so it would need its own ruling.
Restating the contract needs neither.

Two engine defects found alongside, which do not block the channels but are real:

  The authored count of 2 arrives as "at least 2". The cardinality parser short-circuits on EXISTS
  without reading the value, and for a string value falls to a prose path that never consults whether
  the requirement was COUNT or RANGE — so an exact 2 and "2 or more" parse identically. The corrected
  version of that same parse already exists elsewhere in the engine and is never reached for a
  collection row.

  And that cardinality is then dead data. Nothing reads it: a collection row gets no resolution
  line, and a class carrying a selector forms no existential claim. The authored "2" currently
  constrains nothing at all.

  Worth one more line because it is the same clause read two ways: on the exclusion side the engine
  refuses to read a prose count, saying in terms that SD-32 forbids it and guessing would decide the
  item's meaning. On the establishing side it guesses, and guesses wrong.


4 — OPERATIONAL PARTICIPATION: implemented, and the answer is BOTH

The requirement is in as you framed it, and deliberately does not read the `functions` row — a
region can be fully realized by being a scoring target, modifying value, triggering a transition or
being an information subject, and a decorative function string would realize nothing.

On A04: the scoring line participates, via the objective's reference. The three channels participate
in nothing. Hence Q5 fails with three violations and the output is not a runnable coach activity.

Your question was whether the Wide Zone already establishes the relationship somewhere that is
failing to survive, or whether the knowledge genuinely lacks it. It is both, and the split is clean.

**Failing to survive.** The Wide Zone authors a trigger, referents, and four information rules about
the channels. None of them reaches a line at all — they appear in the concrete game, in `open`, and
in `notEstablished` exactly nowhere. A line on a collection-owned property exists only if an element
class exists on the owning collection row, and the object authors no support-capable existence item
on the value-modifier or consequence rows; its information-rule item is ASSUMED, which §3 and SD-83
bar from establishing an element. The contract records the hole itself: "no item, since V7 cannot
select this object's modifier."

I verified the consequence by injecting the missing existence and rerunning: lines go from 39 to 56,
unestablished rows from 8 to 17, and Gate A's knowledge verdict flips from PASS to FAIL — on the
modifier's magnitude, reported as a declared gap. **So A04's current knowledge pass is partly bought
by those claims vanishing.** That is the same shape as the roster and the region functions, now three
times.

**Genuinely lacking.** Even with the enumeration fixed, the relationship could not be established.
Your own declarations say so: the modifier row is NOT_AUTHORED with the note "whether the advantage
is a modifier (no default among three), frequency or cap, team or side symmetry", and the magnitude
row is CLAIMED and NOT_AUTHORED in the same breath — "no multiplier size or bonus points". The two
authored, required, support-capable items in the whole cluster are a trigger (region entry into a
wide channel) and its referents (both wide channels).

Put plainly: **the channel has an authored trigger and no authored effect.** Something happens when a
player enters it, and nothing says what. That is the authoring decision, and it is not one the engine
can take — the knowledge states there is no default among the three candidates.

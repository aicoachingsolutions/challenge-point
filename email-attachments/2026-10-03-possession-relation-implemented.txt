THE GAP THIS EXPOSED — and it blocks assignment, not just Wide Zone

**The established teams have no identity to refer to.**

The choice space is "one of the teams the game establishes", and two things are true at once. Before
realization the teams do not exist — the existential claim has a shortfall of two, and `performers.teams` is
absent from the resolved game. After realization they exist and are **indistinguishable**: both are
`{satisfies, outfieldCount: 6, goalkeeper: 0}`. Nothing tells them apart but their position in the collection,
which is an engine artifact rather than knowledge.

So possession cannot be *assigned* even where it is established, because there is nothing to assign it to.
`teamDesignations` contemplates `TEAM_<id> (a named team)` and no contracted item authors a name.

I think this is a direct and correct consequence of removing the designations, and it clarifies what they were
doing: **they were standing in for team identity as well as for possession, without authority for either.**
Removing them was right, and it left the identity question visible where it had been hidden.

Two further consequences worth naming. The possession choice cannot be a pre-realization freedom at all, because
its choice space is empty until realization instantiates the members — the same shape as the roster, which
became a post-realization entailment. And identity is a different question from possession: a team needs to be
referrable before anything can say which one holds the ball.

I have not designed anything for it, per your instruction to report rather than repair. It is the obvious next
question, and it is smaller than this one was.

THE RELATIONAL CASE — THE EXISTING MECHANISM CANNOT EXPRESS IT

I checked before proposing, as you asked. relationshipRules is a precedence
mechanism — "decides X against Y, outcome Z". COMPARES is closer, and has the
right shape (it takes no line and lives in the reconciliation record), but its
form is two operands, and pairwise distinctness across a set whose members are
not individuated cannot be written as a fixed pair.

Smallest proposal: one assertion on the collection row, DISTINCT_ON — over a
named set, members must differ pairwise on a stated list of field rows. No line,
no metric, no relational geometry. Realization reads it as a joint validity
condition. For our case that is one assertion: candidates must differ pairwise
on O4 and O5.

Meanwhile VARTARGET-03.a is marked relational: it bounds nothing and bars
per-line freedom on the three lines it reaches. The flattening is undone without
my asserting a replacement.

IE-C006 AND IE-D006 — WHY THE OBJECT CANNOT CHOOSE

They are not alternatives. IE-C006 is a NONCANONICAL_PRESET that composes
dimensions; IE-D006 is a canonical dimension definition with seven allowed
values and an integrity condition ("the trigger must be detectable and causally
connected to the revealed information; external signals require additional
scrutiny").

IE-C006 in full: "Revelation state dependency: objective dependent; trigger:
state transition or opponent action; reveal progression: immediate or staged."

So it settles ONE of the five dimensions the object needs (state dependency),
NARROWS two without choosing (trigger, progression), and is silent on two
(reveal timing — which is exactly what the restatement recorded — and access
holder). And it is non-canonical.

The underlying decision is therefore not "C006 or D006". It is: is IE-C006
promoted to canonical and made to state values for every revelation dimension
the representation requires, or must a Variable Target object state each
IE-D0xx dimension individually?

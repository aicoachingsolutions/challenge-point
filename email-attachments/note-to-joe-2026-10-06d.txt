TWO PLACES I CORRECTED MYSELF, BOTH BEFORE SENDING

1. The possession swap. I ran it, got the clean result, and was about to report it as evidence. It
   isn't: the test cannot fail on A04, because nothing in the rendering or fidelity path reads
   possession at all — deleting the relation entirely gives a byte-identical activity. So the
   counterfactual measures our wiring, not the holder's meaning. The classification he asked about is
   still right, but it has to be argued from the register's own definition of the relation as state,
   not from the experiment. I said that plainly rather than burying it.

2. What objects[] counts. I was going to tell him the evidence settles on "functional objects". It
   doesn't, and the error was a quantifier: the spec says every object a rule REFERENCES exists,
   which is a floor, not a ceiling. And the Variable Target Condition authors the opposite outright —
   candidate targets the live rule does not key on must still be present, "so the choice remains
   open". His own ratified boundary has both limbs in it. The question is genuinely open and it's his;
   the useful part is that it doesn't need settling to leave the count alone.

Both corrections came out of running an adversarial pass over my own conclusions rather than only
over the agents'. That was worth the time — the first one would have been a real misreport.

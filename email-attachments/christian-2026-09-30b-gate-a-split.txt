WHAT THE SPLIT IS

Gate A now reports two verdicts over the same checks. The knowledge verdict
ranges over every clause knowledge alone can answer, and it is what gates
realization. The overall verdict ranges over all of them and is never PASS while
anything is still owed, so a deferred clause cannot be lost by the split. Each
deferred clause names what realization must supply, and the resolved game carries
that list — deferred, not waived.


THE CORRECTION

This morning I told you four Gate A checks could not be evaluated before
realization, and proposed deferring them. I implemented that, A04 became
eligible, and I was about to report a first realized game.

Two of the four do not survive scrutiny.

GA-ENVELOPE-FIT cannot compare a placement because the corpus authors placements
as PROSE: "touchline-adjacent", "the full axis extent, end line to end line",
"attacking end ... touches an end line, not the interior". A realized value drawn
from such a bound is the same prose. §1.9 can no more compare it after
realization than before. The same is true of GA-ONE-PRIMARY-EVENT's referent
position clause, and of GA-LAYOUT-FEASIBLE, which cannot read a qualitative bound
and will not be able to read the value chosen from it either.

So those three clauses are blocked by a REPRESENTATION limitation, not a
sequencing one. Deferring them would have promised a later check that cannot run
— which is the same shape as suppressing a required property, arrived at from the
other direction. I reverted them to NOT_EVALUABLE with the reason stated in
place.

Only two clauses are genuinely settled by realization:

  GA-ONE-PRIMARY-EVENT   the primary-event kind, which realization chooses from
                         the set the selection narrowed to
  GA-ROSTER-SUM          whose terms do not exist until realization instantiates
                         the teams SD-97 says are asserted and individuated by
                         nothing

A04 is therefore NOT eligible, and there is now a test saying so and naming the
three blocked clauses, so this cannot quietly read as progress it is not.

THREE DEFECTS THE RUN FOUND

Running it on a real game found three things the fixtures never would have:

  - `nothingInvented` descended INTO derived values, so the three fields of a
    typed structural reference were each reported as an invention. Three
    violations, every one of them a value the run had derived. It now stops at an
    accounted path — a derived value's internal shape is part of that value.

  - instantiated members were placed at the literal key "objectives[]", beside
    the derived `objectives`. Two collections where the game has one, so anything
    reading `objectives` would have missed every member realization supplied.
    That is the silent-loss shape exactly, and it was in the output I nearly sent
    you.

  - a claim's cardinality was not checked at all. "Two teams exist" was satisfied
    by instantiating one. Now refused, with a test.

And one caught by an invariant that already existed: a check with a deferred
clause was reading as PASS while naming the lines it was blocked on. The "no check
passes while blocked" test caught it immediately.


THE REAL REMAINING BLOCKER

Geometry is authored qualitatively, and every spatial check needs it numerically.
That is what stands between us and a first eligible game — not knowledge, and not
sequencing.

Two ways I can see, and I am not choosing between them:

  (a) Authored metric bounds where geometry matters, so §1.9 has numbers to
      compare. That is authoring, and it is the thing you have consistently
      declined to invent — "no scaling rule for width", "no distance, proportion
      or width rule".

  (b) A representation for qualitative placement that the spatial checks can
      reason over — "touchline-adjacent" as a typed relation to a boundary rather
      than a phrase. Then the checks answer a different question ("is this
      relation satisfiable in the envelope?") and never need a metre value.

(b) looks to me like the smaller change and the one more faithful to how the
knowledge is actually written, but it is a representation decision and it is
yours. (a) would need you to author numbers you have said the sources do not
supply.

Everything else stands where the last note left it: the goal-kick unit and the
four Variable Target dimensions are yours to author, and neither blocks this.

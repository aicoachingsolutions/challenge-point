THE THING TO SETTLE FIRST: THIS REVERSES YOUR 2 OCTOBER TRAVERSAL RULING

A ball that crosses the channel and is collected by a teammate beyond it satisfies the new meaning exactly —
access occurred, the defending team did not take over. Your 2 October settled definition excludes it, because
nothing touched the ball *inside* the channel.

So the revision has not only removed the player grain, the control judgement and the remain-in-region
requirement. **It has also made pure traversal-with-retention qualify**, which you previously ruled out. That
may be what you intend — your danger-area example is about the attack reaching consequential space, and a ball
played through to a teammate arguably does that. But it is a change, and I would rather you made it
deliberately than inherited it from a rewording.


WHAT IS GENUINELY GOOD ABOUT THIS VERSION

**Your own authored source is already means-neutral, so none of your non-claims requires broadening anything.**
The Wide Zone setup sentence reads *"Actions starting in or moving through the wide channel earn an
advantage"*. The subject is **"Actions"** — not a pass, not a player, not a team. Nothing has to be stretched
to accommodate a dribble, a recovery or a deflection.

And `REGION_ENTRY` is genuinely means-neutral, verified rather than assumed: it has **no trigger-semantics
entry at all**, the engine constructs it purely from the region classes, and the only two places that parse its
parameterised form discard the argument. It is also subject-silent. So nothing in it privileges a means.

That is a real improvement on the last two candidates, and it serves the ecological intent you stated.


WHERE IT BREAKS

**1. The access half is nearly owned, and falls short by one disjunct.** Your authored sentence has two —
*"starting in or moving through"* — and an authored item has already graded what the token covers:
`WIDEZONE-11.b` records that *"REGION_ENTRY holds 'moves through' only; 'starts in' has no trigger
(VOCABULARY)"*. So of your five enumerated means, a **recovery of a ball already inside the area** is the one
not covered: nothing crosses in during that possession. Small, but it is one of your own five, and it is yours
to rule rather than the token's to decide by silence.

I therefore grade `REGION_ENTRY` as **adjacent precedent, not ownership** — on the discipline you set, because
an authored item has already said the token covers a proper subset of the knowledge.

**2. The team conjunct is not merely unsupported — it is vacuous.** `ATTACKING_TEAM` is glossed as *"the team
the possession relation currently holds"*. So "the access happens while the attacking team possesses" is true
at **every** access, by construction. And the value modifier has no team field at all — the beneficiary field
was deliberately removed. The conjunct carries no information and nothing could carry it.

**3. The success half is blocked three ways, and the first is your own authored knowledge.**

- **A possession change may not be tied to a region.** `GF4 / I07`, row T1c, AUTHORED, EXCLUSION: *"any region
  qualifier (forbidden: the transition moment is not tied to a region)"*, from *"possession change at any
  point"*. So "the defending team took over **through that access**" cannot be written as a region-qualified
  possession change. No decision about when access resolves clears this; it is authored against.
- **"The defending team takes over" has no field.** `awardedTo` is applicable only when play stops, and every
  authored possession change in the corpus continues play — so that row is withdrawn as inapplicable.
- **The representation cannot state the absence of an event at all.** The selector language has no negation,
  and the negative constructs that exist — `NOT_EXISTS` and `EXCLUSION` — are evaluated over the element
  classes a game **contains**, never over what **happens**. Probed three ways on the live game and it behaves
  exactly as that description implies.

**4. The elegant candidate is circular.** I tested "the access and the attack fall in the same episode", because
it looked like it needed no new boundary decision — the boundary would be the possession change itself, which
is precisely what you said not to invent.

It does not work. *"Within the same episode"* asks whether a possession change occurred between the access and
the end of the episode — but **the end of the episode is that possession change.** The predicate is vacuously
true of every access, and your second case would be awarded the bonus. The variant that anchors on the score
instead fails for a different reason: **a score itself begins a new episode**, so an access followed by a goal
would fall outside "the same episode" and scoring would void the bonus.

The only repair is to require something *after* the access and *before* the boundary — which is "a required
subsequent action", explicitly one of your non-claims.


SO: IS IT INHERENTLY TEMPORAL? YES

The strongest non-temporal reading is that the access and the possession state are evaluated at the same
moment. It fails your second case, and fails it for a reason internal to the representation rather than to
football: possession moves **only** at a possession change, so at the instant the ball crosses, possession is
still the attacking team's in **both** your cases. They are indistinguishable at a single instant and differ
only in what follows.

Your own wording carried the temporality all along and did not lose it in the revision — *"survives the
access"*, *"through that attempted access"*, and 2 October's *"after which"*. What the revision removed was the
player grain, the control judgement and the remain-in-region requirement. Not the time relation.

**Canonical authority for *a* temporal relation exists**: exactly one entailing item, your 29 September
pass-reveal ruling, which owns point-succession of an information reveal anchored to one named trigger. No
interval, no second event, no non-occurrence. **Authority for *this* relation does not exist anywhere.**


THE SMALLEST GAP — and it is smaller and better-located than the field you declined

**A value modifier's condition cannot name an occurrence.** The referent row's registered type is already
*"references to regions, objects **or events**"*, and the condition-type vocabulary already contains `event`.
So the slot exists.

What is missing is **identity for an event referent** — and the corpus shows exactly where the line falls.
`GF4 / I15` authors an event-typed condition with the referents *"{regain, shot}, in the order regain then
shot"*. Its own structural clause says: *"The referent set is structural. **The order … is not held.**"* So the
representation can already **name** events in a modifier condition; it cannot **relate** them. Relating them is
the whole of what Successful Region Access needs.

And then, second, nothing can say two occurrences fall in the same episode: an episode exists only as a boolean
on a transition, and the standing decision that defines episode boundaries says in terms that it *"defines
boundaries only; entails no T1 element."*

I am not proposing either. You asked for the gap and that is the gap, in the representation's own vocabulary.


YOUR WIDE ZONE EXCLUSIONS DO NOT CONFLICT

**None of the three contradicts the revised meaning, and none needs revising.** The one that looked dangerous is
not: `WIDEZONE-08.a` forbids `access` as a member of a region's **functions** — and its own fitNote says what
that means: *"Restricted, exclusive or entry-prohibited zone = an access function on the channel; forbidden."*
That is access **control** — a zone players may be kept out of — which is the opposite of your meaning. Its
evidence is *"Channels are reference markers — players can choose to use them or not."*

The other two forbid a wide channel as a referent on the consequence row and on the primary-event condition row.
The advantage lives on the value modifier instead, which is where your 2 October ruling put it, so neither is
touched. **The meaning belongs exactly where it already is; what is missing is a row, not a permission.**

is checked by neither. Reported, not repaired; it joins the reachability issue you already hold.
